# cida-type4

Use this short forced skill for CIDA / VocabGo Type 4 questions: collocation multi-select and phrase-fill word tiles.

Keywords: CIDA, VocabGo, app.vocabgo.com, Type 4, fourth question type, collocation, multi-select, phrase fill, word tiles, preface, brief, write, remarks, book, bail, on bail, Choose the words commonly used together with, 本题有, 选中, 还需选中, 剩余机会, 回答正确, 继续, 删除, left arrow.

## Collocation Multi-Select

- Prompt pattern: `Choose the words commonly used together with <target>`.
- Read counters first. `本题有N个答案，选中M个即可通过` means there may be more correct answers than needed. `还需选中 K` means click exactly K more correct chips.
- If `还需选中 0` or the green Continue button is available after enough green checks, stop selecting and click Continue.
- A star on an unselected chip can reveal another correct answer, but do not click it when the required count is already zero.
- For `preface`, good collocations are `brief`, `write`, `remarks`, `book`. A passing set in the example is `write`, `remarks`, `book`. Avoid `environment`, `death`, `communities`.

## Phrase Fill Word Tiles

- Count underline blanks before selecting. Multiple underline groups usually mean a phrase.
- Fill left-to-right. The displayed target word may still need to be selected as one tile.
- The yellow/orange left arrow inside the exercise is Delete/Backspace, not Back, Submit, Continue, or browser return.
- For target `bail` with meaning `保释；保释中`, the correct phrase is `on bail`: tap `on`, then `bail`.
- If `bail` was selected before `on`, tap the yellow/orange delete arrow once, then tap `on`, then `bail`.
- When the screen shows `回答正确`, click the green Continue button.
